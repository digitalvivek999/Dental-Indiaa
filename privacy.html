Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

At Dr. S. Lathasree Dental Clinic ("we", "our", or "us"), patient confidentiality, medical record security, and privacy are paramount. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, and protect personal and health-related data collected via this website and our clinical OPD communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information voluntarily submitted when you schedule an OPD consultation, reach out via WhatsApp, or submit contact forms, including your full name, contact phone/WhatsApp number, preferred date and time slot, and details regarding your dental concern.

2. How We Use Patient Data

Your details are used exclusively to confirm appointment slots, communicate clinical follow-ups, deliver treatment estimates, and provide emergency dental support. We never sell, lease, or distribute patient contact details to third-party commercial marketing brokers.

3. Medical Data Security & Confidentiality

All clinical inquiries and submission forms follow strict healthcare data hygiene standards. Digital records and consultation queries are kept strictly confidential under Indian Medical Ethics & Dental Council protocols.
