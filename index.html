Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Your Dental Questions

Direct, honest, and expert answers to the most common concerns regarding dental treatments, costs, and pain management.

Is a single-sitting root canal really pain-free?
Yes, absolutely. With computerized local anesthesia delivery, advanced apex locators, and rotary nickel-titanium instruments, the infected pulp nerve is anesthetized completely within 60 seconds. Most patients report feeling zero discomfort and fall asleep during the 45-minute procedure.
How long do German & Swiss dental implants last?
Titanium dental implants are engineered to be permanent tooth root replacements. Clinical studies show a 98.4% success rate over 25+ years. With normal brushing, flossing, and semi-annual cleanings, dental implants typically last a lifetime.
Can I eat all foods with invisible clear aligners?
Yes! Unlike traditional metal braces which trap food and prohibit crunchy or sticky snacks, clear aligners are completely removable. You simply take them off before meals, enjoy whatever you love, brush, and snap them back on.
How does the 0% No-Cost EMI payment plan work?
We partner directly with Bajaj Finserv Health, Pine Labs, and major credit cards (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis). The treatment cost is divided into equal 3, 6, or 12 month installments without any interest or hidden processing fees. You pay only the exact treatment amount.
Is laser teeth whitening safe for my tooth enamel?
Yes. In-clinic laser teeth whitening uses a neutral pH gel activated by cold laser wavelengths. It lifts deep staining compounds without eroding enamel crystals, unlike abrasive whitening toothpastes or unregulated online bleaching kits.
